## This site

This site is a set of static files built from the public
[jumplink.github.io](https://github.com/JumpLink/jumplink.github.io) repository. It has no
accounts, no forms, no comments and no newsletter, so it collects nothing you type. It sets no
cookies, embeds no analytics or tracking service and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other
servers. The blog's Atom feeds are static files too.

## Light and dark

If you click the sun or the moon, your browser remembers the chosen scheme in its local storage
(`localStorage`, key `scheme`). The value never leaves your browser and is sent to no one. It is
strictly necessary for a function you explicitly asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Clicking back to
the system setting deletes it; you can also remove it through your browser's site data.
